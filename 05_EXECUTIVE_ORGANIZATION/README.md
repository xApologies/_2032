# Executive organization

Epistemic status: **DESIGN HYPOTHESIS**. Bootstrap date: 2026-09-21.
Source: SRC-0001; generated design elaborations are proposals, not adopted decisions.

## Prospective responsibility model

Model departments, domain experts, coordinators, and decision authorities as roles
with responsibilities, dependencies, decision scope, escalation routes, and evidence
obligations. Do not imply legal powers or reorganizations have been adopted.

For each question record: accountable human owner, consulted domains, implementation
owner, authority basis requiring verification, deadline, dissent, and outcome owner.
Routine matters stay with authorized domains. Cross-domain disagreement goes to a
human coordinator; unresolved questions requiring presidential authority reach the
decision surface with alternatives and preserved dissent. Return insufficient briefs.

Research deliverable: responsibility matrix and dependency map for a synthetic case.
Acceptance: no unowned question, no AI-assigned legal authority, no silent escalation
loss. Legal and institutional review must precede claims about actual powers.
