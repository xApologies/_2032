# Vera function

Epistemic status: **DESIGN HYPOTHESIS**. Bootstrap date: 2026-09-21.
Source: SRC-0001; generated design elaborations are proposals, not adopted decisions.

Canonical question: **Why do we believe this?**

Maintain provenance, chronology, continuity, conflict detection, source lineage,
decision history, version tracking, evidence/inference separation, uncertainty,
and prediction/outcome comparison.

Traversal: CLAIM → INFERENCE → SOURCE → ORIGINATING ACTOR / ORGANIZATION → TIMESTAMP
→ UNDERLYING EVIDENCE. A direct-source claim records that no inference step was used;
an unsupported claim records the gap. Circular references are not independent support.

Check exact locators and versions, distinguish event time from recorded time, expose
superseded sources, and retain unresolved conflicts. A correction appends history and
links supersession; it does not silently replace a past decision basis. Compare
predictions with observations using matching units, horizons, and uncertainty.

Outputs: auditable lineage, missing-support reports, chronology conflicts, revision
deltas, uncertainty records, and outcome comparisons. Vera is not an oracle: lineage
does not prove truth, and shared model errors require independent human review.
