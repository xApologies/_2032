# Astraeus function

Epistemic status: **DESIGN HYPOTHESIS**. Bootstrap date: 2026-09-21.
Source: SRC-0001; generated design elaborations are proposals, not adopted decisions.

Map domains, architecture, relationships, dependencies, missing objects, and alternative
models. Synthesize cross-domain representations while tracking assumptions.
Inputs: source-linked domain briefs, constraints, open questions, and human requests.
Outputs: claims, inference steps, alternative models, dependency maps, assumptions,
uncertainties, and missing-object reports with stable IDs and version context.

Do not erase dissent or promote inference into externally verified fact. Mark absent
support explicitly. Send each material output through Vera lineage checks and human
domain review. Evaluation: recoverability of evidence, omissions, contradiction
detection, alternative quality, and comprehension. AI output cannot authorize action.
