# Human factors

Epistemic status: **DESIGN HYPOTHESIS**. Bootstrap date: 2026-09-21.
Source: SRC-0001; generated design elaborations are proposals, not adopted decisions.

## Operator requirement

The intended operator may reason more efficiently through spoken dialogue while
walking or pacing than through prolonged seated reading. Treat this as an interface
requirement to evaluate, not a diagnosis or universal performance claim.

Support movement, voice, conversation, visual supplementation, primary-source recovery,
human advisers, and persistent context. Briefs should permit progressive depth rather
than require linear reading of large binders. Provide equivalent text, keyboard,
caption, screen-reader, seated, and low-bandwidth modes; retain user control of pace.

## Evaluation proposal

Counterbalance speech/movement and conventional conditions on equivalent scenarios.
Measure comprehension, source recovery, fatigue, interruptions, decision errors,
accessibility barriers, and preference. Avoid speed-only success criteria. Correct
speech recognition errors visibly and confirm consequential intent. Preserve privacy
in recording and observer access. Stop on participant discomfort or unsafe movement.
No superiority claim precedes data; accommodation remains available regardless of averages.
