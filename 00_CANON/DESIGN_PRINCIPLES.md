# Design principles

Epistemic status: **ADOPTED PROJECT DECISION**. Bootstrap date: 2026-09-21.
Source: SRC-0001; generated design elaborations are proposals, not adopted decisions.

1. Human authority: AI retrieves, synthesizes, maps, analyzes documents, detects
   contradictions, supports accessibility and simulation, and preserves memory.
   Humans authorize decisions and remain accountable.
2. Attention is limited: present a usable brief, material dissent, urgency, and
   drill-down sources rather than require linear consumption of briefing binders.
3. Preserve epistemic categories, timestamps, source lineage, and uncertainty.
4. Delegate to responsible experts with explicit escalation and return paths.
5. Make spoken reasoning, movement, visual supplementation, and advisers first-class.
6. Measure comprehension, quality, staff effort, recovery, and outcomes, not novelty.
7. Access is not authority; security capabilities require independent review.
8. Keep implementation choices replaceable and compare simpler architectures.
9. Preserve corrections, conflicting evidence, and failed predictions.
10. Redesign or reject architectures that fail relevant baseline comparisons.
