# Project charter

Epistemic status: **ADOPTED PROJECT DECISION**. Bootstrap date: 2026-09-21.
Source: SRC-0001; generated design elaborations are proposals, not adopted decisions.

## Purpose and boundaries

Research a prospective 2032 U.S. presidential-administration architecture through
design, public or synthetic simulation, evaluation, and transparent provenance.
This project does not assert public office, candidacy, government endorsement,
clearance, appointments, or anyone's consent to prospective personnel roles.

## Roles and authority

Genesis: Project Director / Systems Architect; directs project scope and adoption.
Astraeus: mapping, architecture, synthesis, and dependency discovery.
Vera: provenance, chronology, continuity, conflict detection, and audit.
The latter two are functional roles, not officeholders or holders of government authority.
Project approval is not governmental authorization. Access is not authority.

## Success and scope

Improve decision quality, presidential attention, information throughput, provenance,
institutional memory, expert delegation, cross-domain synthesis, accessibility,
human-AI collaboration, and measurable outcomes. Compare against simpler workflows.
Research includes Clover and VA institutional improvement; no population-level
conclusion may rest on personal experience alone.

## Change control

Record proposals, evidence, alternatives, assumptions, uncertainties, and dissent.
Only an explicit human decision may adopt a project proposal. Record who authorized
what, in which scope, when, and on what basis; append superseding decisions rather
than rewriting history. Merge and adoption are separate events. This bootstrap
does not authorize merging its pull request.
