# VA institutional improvement

Epistemic status: **DESIGN HYPOTHESIS**. Bootstrap date: 2026-09-21.
Source: SRC-0001; generated design elaborations are proposals, not adopted decisions.

## Objective

Develop evidence-based proposals intended to leave the Department of Veterans Affairs
safer, more effective, more accountable, and more useful to veterans.

## Research coverage

Patient safety; medication management; adverse-event response; continuity of care;
mental-health continuity; disability/benefits interfaces; accountability; independent
oversight; error disclosure; veteran redress; staffing; EHR interoperability;
veteran-reported outcomes; and clinical outcomes.

## Evidence-to-proposal workflow

Label personal experience PERSONAL RECOLLECTION; derive a bounded hypothesis and
seek broader evidence before population-level conclusions. Build an evidence table
with source population, date, methods, limitations, contrary findings, and applicability.
Include veteran participation and qualified clinical, operational, and privacy review.
No medical guidance or claims about current VA performance are established here.

Initial deliverable: public/synthetic continuity-of-care scenario with missing handoff
and medication-list discrepancy. Compare detection, time to accountable follow-up,
unresolved issues, burden, and error disclosure. Clinical outcomes require suitable
study design and longer follow-up; simulated detection is not evidence of patient benefit.
Use no identifiable clinical records. Track alternative explanations and potential harms.
