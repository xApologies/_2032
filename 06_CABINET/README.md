# Cabinet

Epistemic status: **DESIGN HYPOTHESIS**. Bootstrap date: 2026-09-21.
Source: SRC-0001; generated design elaborations are proposals, not adopted decisions.

## Prospective domain interfaces

Develop role requirements and expert review interfaces, not personnel appointments.
Any future named-person research must distinguish consideration, contact, consent,
nomination, and appointment; this bootstrap establishes none of them.

A domain brief includes mandate questions, accountable role, primary evidence,
alternatives, dissent, assumptions, resource ranges, interdepartmental dependencies,
implementation constraints, and observable outcomes. Keep professional expertise
and authority distinct. Department organization and legal powers require sourced review.

Initial deliverable: an anonymous domain-expert brief template exercised against a
public or synthetic scenario. Evaluate responsiveness, evidence completeness,
cross-domain conflict detection, and decision-maker comprehension.
